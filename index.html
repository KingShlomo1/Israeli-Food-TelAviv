Our Story

A Neighborhood Favorite Near the Tayelet

Israeli Food Tel Aviv is a small, cozy gyro restaurant tucked into Allenby Street, just a couple of blocks from the Tel Aviv beach promenade. Guests describe it as feeling like a family gathering — warm, simple, and unpretentious, with portions generous enough to share.

We're proud to be a women-owned business serving the dishes Tel Aviv does best: hand-carved shawarma, fresh-fried falafel, and a self-serve bar of pickled eggplant and peppers that regulars come back for again and again.
